## When to use
At the start of a marketing-scoped goal, before any tagging or content work.

## Steps
1. Restate the campaign objective and target audience.
2. Choose channels (e.g. email, paid social, landing page) and a timeline.
3. Draft core messaging/positioning for the campaign.
4. Note what needs tracking (see `implement-tracking-tags`) to measure success.

## Done when
- A GTM plan exists that content and tagging work can be scoped against.
